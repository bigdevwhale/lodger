# Lodger

Someone already lives in your flat. Every night they leave a trace: wet footprints in your hallway photo, a chair that wasn't there, a note at 03:12, a word caught between radio stations. Piece the clues together on a cork board, find out who they were and what they never finished — then do that one real thing for them, and they can finally leave.

A quiet, slow-burning haunting for the Stillcraft collection: no jump-scare treadmill, just the steady feeling that you are not alone at home.

## Design prototype

`design/prototype.html` — open it in a browser (it reads `design/stories.js`).

- **Квартира** — room photos (drawn here; the app uses your own shots from the system camera) where traces appear night by night.
- **Записки** — the lodger's notes, delivered as notifications.
- **Радио** — tune the dial through static; hold on a frequency until a word comes through.
- **Доска** — clues pinned and tied with red thread; when all clues and words are found, the story assembles and the unfinished task is revealed.

The side panel switches lodgers, nights and fear level (Candle / Lantern / Full moon).

## Lodgers

Ten stories in `design/stories.js`: a music teacher waiting for the last chord, a house spirit nobody invited along, a game of hide-and-seek that never ended, a reflection still waiting since June 1941, a postman with one undelivered letter, a kikimora living in an unfinished scarf, the whisperer in the ventilation shaft, the astronomer upstairs who missed Halley's comet, a hostess setting a table for twelve, and a cat who was never given a name.
