## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-24

### Added
- Your own rooms, photographed with the phone's camera or picked from the gallery; rooms the flat doesn't have hand their marks next door.
- Ten lodgers in English and Russian, fourteen nights each, with three empty nights between them.
- Marks painted into the photos themselves — prints, wet steps, a handprint, breath writing, frost, a shadow figure, eyes, a chair, crumbs, a thread, keys, a letter, a cat — placed by reading each photo for its dark corner, glass and floor; new ones develop when the room is opened.
- Evening notes as notifications, never at night.
- The radio: live static, words caught by holding the dial, a low synthesised voice.
- The cork board of clues with red thread, the story, a real task, the farewell, and the house register.
- Fear levels (Candle, Lantern, Full moon), knocks and flicker, first-visit hints, community links, demo build.
